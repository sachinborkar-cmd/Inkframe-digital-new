Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

What we collect

When you buy an ebook or send us a message we collect your name, email address, and any details you choose to include. Payment details are handled entirely by our payment provider and never reach our servers.

Why we collect it

To deliver your files, resend download links, answer support requests, and — only if you opt in — tell you when a new book is out.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent your data. We share it only with the service providers needed to run the store: payment processing, email delivery, and form handling.

Cookies

We use only what is necessary to keep the site working. We do not run advertising trackers.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email us and we'll act within 30 days.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Use the contact page.
