About

A small press with one rule: cut it in half.

Inkframe Press publishes short, practical ebooks for people who do the work. Most business books are one good idea stretched across three hundred pages. We'd rather ship ninety pages you finish, mark up, and actually use.

Every title goes through the same process: a working system, a group of test readers who run it for a month, then an edit that removes anything that didn't survive contact with a real week. If a chapter doesn't change what someone does on Monday, it doesn't ship.

Reader-funded

DRM-free forever

Your files are yours. Copy them, print them, keep them.

Priced for India

Rupee-first pricing, because that's where most of our readers are.
